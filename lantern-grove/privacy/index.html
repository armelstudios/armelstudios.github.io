About this policy

This policy explains how the Android game Lantern Grove (package name com.armelstudios.lanterngrove, distributed through Google Play) handles information about you. It covers the app itself and any messages you send us about it.

We use "we", "us" and "our" for the developer described below, and "you" for anyone who plays the game. The game is available in English, Spanish and Russian. This policy is written in English.

We are also responsible for choosing to include Google's advertising technology in the game. Google is responsible for what it does with the data it collects through that technology (see "Advertising and Google" below).

Information saved on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the game saves the following on your device:

  • puzzle progress and best scores
  • garden restoration progress
  • petals, the in-game currency, which you can only earn by playing and which has no real-money value
  • cosmetic themes you have unlocked
  • your settings: sound, music, haptics (vibration), reduced motion and language
  • your daily challenge history
  • which story notes you have already seen
  • a list of reward receipt numbers, so that a rewarded ad cannot give you the same reward twice

The reward receipt numbers are random values generated on your device. They are not linked to your name, your Google Account, your advertising ID or any other identifier.

This information is stored in a single file, lantern_grove.json, inside the game's private storage area on your device. The game also keeps a backup copy and a temporary copy of that file, so a save interrupted by a crash or a dead battery does not corrupt your progress. Other apps cannot read this storage area.

This information never leaves your device through the game. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Lantern Grove, so the game does not upload this file to your Google Drive backup.

Because the data exists only on your device, we cannot access it, recover it if it is lost, move it to a new device, or delete it remotely.

Information we collect

Through the game: none. Lantern Grove has no accounts, sign-in, email collection, purchases, cloud saves, chat, user-generated content, analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. We do not operate servers that the game connects to.

When you contact us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply and to handle your request.

From Google. As the publisher of the game, we can see reports that Google provides to app developers through Google Play Console and AdMob, such as numbers of installs, ad impressions and earnings, and technical crash or performance reports. These reports are aggregated or technical and do not tell us who you are. Google collects the underlying information under its own privacy policy.

Google Play reviews. If you post a public review of the game on Google Play, we can see your review and the public name Google Play displays with it, and we may reply publicly.

Advertising and Google

Lantern Grove is free because it shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (software from Google built into the game through an open-source plugin for the Godot game engine).

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • Interstitial ads, full-screen ads shown after some completed puzzles.
  • Rewarded ads, which are optional. You choose whether to watch one to earn 20 petals.

What the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects

When the game requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device and sends it to Google. According to Google's own disclosure for the Android SDK, this includes:

  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate the general location of your device (for example, the city or region). The game does not have permission to use your device's precise location (GPS).
  • Device identifiers: your device's Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers Google describes in its disclosure.
  • Product interactions: for example app launches, taps and ad video views.
  • Diagnostic information: app and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Ad requests also carry technical information needed to choose and display an ad, such as the name of the app and details of your device and its operating system. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit.

You can read Google's disclosure here: Google Mobile Ads SDK (Android) data disclosure.

How Google uses this information

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. That includes choosing and delivering ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad performance, reporting to us and to advertisers in aggregated form, detecting invalid traffic and fraud, and improving its services. Where you allow it, Google also uses this information to personalise the ads you see.

Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, How Google uses cookies in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Personalised ads are chosen using information about your past activity, for example in other apps and on websites that use Google's services. They rely on identifiers such as your advertising ID.

Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour. Google chooses them from the context, such as the app you are using, and your coarse current location (for example, your city). Google states that non-personalised ads may still use identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting and fraud prevention.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, if you do not agree to personalised or non-personalised ads, Google may show limited ads. These do not use personal data for personalisation. They still use your IP address to deliver the ad, and Google may still use limited information stored on your device to detect fraud and abuse.

Google's role

Google acts as an independent controller of the personal data it collects through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, under the Google Ads Data Protection Terms. This means Google decides how it uses that data, and Google's privacy policy and controls apply to it. We do not receive the data that the SDK sends to Google.

Google states that it does not sell personal information. For more on Google's approach, see Google's information on US state privacy laws.

Your advertising choices

Consent message and Advertising privacy choices

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required, the game shows Google's consent message when you start it, and ads are requested only after your choices allow it. You can accept, decline or choose specific purposes.
  • Other regions: we may show a privacy message where state or national law requires one, including for some US states.

When a privacy message applies to you, Settings → Advertising privacy choices appears in the game. Use it at any time to review your choices or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. If you do not see that option, no privacy message applies in your region, and you can use the device and Google controls below.

Your device's advertising ID

You can control the advertising ID on your Android device for all apps:

  • Delete it: on recent Android versions, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path can differ by device) and choose Delete advertising ID. Apps then receive a string of zeros instead of the ID, and ads cannot be personalised using it.
  • Reset it: in the same place, choose Reset advertising ID to replace it with a new one. This breaks the link with the information associated with your old ID.
  • Older Android versions: turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization in the ads settings.

Google explains these options in its Advertising ID help article.

Google's own controls

  • My Ad Center lets you turn ad personalisation on or off and manage the information Google uses for ads, including from apps that use Google's services.
  • If you are signed in to a Google Account, you can review your activity in My Activity and your settings in Google Account.

Turning off personalisation does not remove ads from the game. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.

Why we process information and our legal bases

This section is for players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where we must explain the legal basis for each use of personal data.

  • Saving your progress and settings on your device. We store this to provide the game you chose to play (performance of a contract) and because storing it is strictly necessary for the game to work. This data stays on your device.
  • Granting rewards and preventing double rewards. Giving you the petals you chose to earn is part of providing the game (contract). Keeping reward receipt numbers to stop a reward being granted twice is in our legitimate interest in keeping the in-game economy fair.
  • Personalised ads. Only with your consent, given through the consent message. Where the law requires consent to store or read information on your device for advertising, that consent is collected through the same message.
  • Non-personalised and limited ads, ad measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention. These rely on legitimate interests: ours in funding a free game through advertising, and Google's in measuring ads and keeping its services secure. Where the law requires consent for any of these uses (for example for storing or reading information on your device), Google relies on the consent you give through the consent message.
  • Replying to your messages. Our legitimate interest in answering you and handling your request, or our legal obligations when your message is a request to exercise your privacy rights.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests (see "Your rights").

For transfers out of the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on adequacy decisions, the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks (including the UK Extension), and standard contractual clauses, as described in Google's data transfer frameworks.

If messages you send us are stored or handled outside your country, we use appropriate safeguards required by law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long information is kept

  • Data saved on your device stays there until you clear the game's storage or uninstall the game.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is kept for the periods Google sets, described in How Google retains data.
  • Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and deleted within two years of our last exchange, unless we must keep them longer to meet a legal obligation or to establish or defend legal claims.

Security

The game's save files sit in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The game does not send these files anywhere. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. We take reasonable steps to protect the email account we use for player correspondence.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device can open the game, so please use your device's screen lock.

Android permissions

The game asks Android only for these permissions:

  • Internet and network state, to request and show ads and check whether a connection is available.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so it can read your advertising ID, subject to your device settings.
  • Vibrate, for optional haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

US state privacy rights

This section applies to residents of California and other US states with consumer privacy laws, such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and Oregon.

What is collected. In the past 12 months, the following categories of personal information have been collected through the game, all by Google's Mobile Ads SDK from your device:

  • Identifiers: IP address, advertising ID, app set ID
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: ad and app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views, and diagnostic data
  • Approximate geolocation, inferred from your IP address (not precise location)

It is used for the purposes described in "How Google uses this information" and disclosed to Google. If you email us, we also receive your email address and your message, which we use only to respond. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by these laws.

Sale, sharing and targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. We treat it that way and give you these ways to opt out:

  • Settings → Advertising privacy choices in the game, where a US state privacy message is shown
  • deleting your device's advertising ID, or opting out of ads personalisation, in Android settings (see "Your device's advertising ID")
  • turning off personalised ads in My Ad Center
  • emailing armel.studios.support@gmail.com with the subject "Do not sell or share"

We do not use personal information for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16.

Children

Lantern Grove is not directed to children. In Google Play it is aimed at players aged 13 and over.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the higher age of digital consent that applies in your country. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone.

If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country, please ask a parent or guardian to make the advertising choices in the game.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the game, you can:

  • uninstall the game or clear its storage, which deletes all game data
  • reset or delete the device's advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation, as described above
  • email us at armel.studios.support@gmail.com. We will delete any correspondence from your child and help you with anything else we reasonably can.

How to delete your data

There is no account to delete, and we keep no player data on servers. Here is how to remove each type of information:

1. Game data on your device (progress, scores, petals, themes, settings, daily history, story notes and reward receipts)

  • Uninstall Lantern Grove, or
  • open Android Settings → Apps → Lantern Grove → Storage (the wording can differ by device) and tap Clear storage or Clear data.

Either way, all game data, including the backup and temporary copies, is deleted from your device straight away. Because automatic cloud backup is turned off for the game, no copy is kept in your Google Drive backup. This cannot be undone, and we cannot restore it.

2. Advertising data held by Google

  • reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (see "Your device's advertising ID")
  • turn off ad personalisation and manage your data in My Ad Center
  • if you are signed in to a Google Account, delete activity in My Activity
  • for any other request about data Google holds, use the options in the Google Privacy Policy

Google retains and deletes this data under its own policies.

3. Messages you have sent us

Email armel.studios.support@gmail.com and ask us to delete your correspondence. We will delete it within one month, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation or to establish or defend legal claims, and we will tell you if that applies.
